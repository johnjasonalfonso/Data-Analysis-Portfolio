Patient Costing Analysis: Qlik Sense Dashboard

Project Goal

This project uses Qlik Sense to explore healthcare costs and patient utilization patterns. The dashboard brings together executive-level summaries, geographic analysis, and patient behavior insights to support data-driven review of healthcare spending.

View Project on GitHub  |  Download Qlik Sense App (.qvf)

To open the downloaded QVF file, use Qlik Sense Desktop or a compatible Qlik Sense environment.

Dashboard Overview

The dashboard is organized into three analytical views:

  • CEO Overview: High-level summary of patient costing metrics.
  • Geo Analytics: Geographic patterns in healthcare costs and utilization.
  • Behaviour Analysis: Patient behavior patterns related to cost and utilization.
