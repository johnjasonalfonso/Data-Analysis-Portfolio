## Featured Projects

### 1. Statistical Analysis – Food Bacteria Study

An R-based statistical analysis of bacterial counts in chilled and non-chilled food samples, exploring distributions, differences between groups, and variation across temperature categories.

**Methods and Tools**
- Data cleaning and exploratory data analysis
- Descriptive statistics and data visualization
- Shapiro-Wilk normality testing
- Wilcoxon rank-sum testing
- Kruskal-Wallis testing and pairwise comparisons

**Project Files:** [View Food Bacteria Analysis](R/Food-Bacteria-Analysis/)

### 2. Collections Analytics Framework

Developed an analytical framework using R to evaluate collection productivity, customer contactability, payment behavior, and recovery performance across delinquency buckets.

The analysis covered historical collections data from June 2025 to March 2026 and supported recommendations around early-stage collections prioritization and automated outreach opportunities.

**Methods and Tools**
- Data preparation and integration using R
- Collection productivity and contactability metrics
- Payment behavior and recovery analysis
- Data visualization and executive summary reporting

**Project Files:** [View Collections Analytics Framework](R/Collections-Analytics-Framework/)

**Methods and Tools**

- CTEs and window functions
- Data integration and aggregation
- KPI development and portfolio segmentation

- Reporting automation

### 4. Business Intelligence and Data Visualization

### 5. Dengue and Climate Statistical Research

Applied statistical research exploring relationships between dengue incidence and climate variables, including temperature, rainfall, relative humidity, and heat index.

The project applies statistical and spatio-temporal analysis to investigate climate-related patterns in dengue incidence.

**Methods and Tools**
- Descriptive and exploratory data analysis
- Spearman correlation analysis
- Principal Component Analysis (PCA)
- Clustering and heatmap visualization
- Time-series decomposition
- Mann-Kendall trend testing and Theil-Sen slope estimation
- Climate and ENSO-based analysis
